P·01UAE Digital TwinMINISTRY OF INTERIOR · 2023 — PRESENT
Flagship · Tahaluf Al Emarat · Future Simulation

A country,
rebuilt in real time

A national-scale digital twin of the United Arab Emirates — georeferenced 3D cities running in Unreal Engine, where police assets, incidents and infrastructure live as interactive layers over an Arabic-first command interface. Mohamad Hashem builds the cities themselves: from satellite data to optimized, textured, geolocated urban fabric.

3ds MaxUnreal EngineDatasmithGISSubstanceArabic / English UI

The platform covers the seven Emirates. Each district is reconstructed at LOD 100–200 from satellite and GIS footprints, classified into eight building categories, then pushed through Datasmith into an Unreal Engine world with live dashboards, day-night cycle and event simulation.

Mohamad Hashem's role: the 3D city pipeline end-to-end — footprint extraction, low-poly modeling, UV mapping, texturing, geolocation, naming and tagging, collision, optimization, and delivery into engine.

3.1

Al Tall Regeneration — UNDP

A UNDP-backed regeneration of the Ottoman clock-tower square at the heart of Tripoli. Mohamad Hashem authored the full 3D reconstruction and every visualisation, then ran the science underneath: solar comfort, wind, shadow and pedestrian simulations that shaped where people would actually sit, walk and stay.

3ds MaxCoronaLadybug / HoneybeePhotogrammetry

The model was rebuilt from drone photogrammetry of the real district; proposals were tested against annual comfort and wind data before a single render was framed. All modeling and all renders on this page are Mohamad Hashem's.

3.2

The evidence layer

Design decisions were argued with data — annual comfort zones, solstice shadows, wind velocity, pedestrian stay-places, commercial heat — down to algorithmically placed café locations scored against the comfort map. Some layers were built the slow way — walking the site, counting, measuring, then turning the field notes into diagrams; others were simulated computationally in Grasshopper with Ladybug and Honeybee.
