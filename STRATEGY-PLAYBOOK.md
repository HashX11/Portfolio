# HASHEM DANKAR — PORTFOLIO IDENTITY & STRATEGY PLAYBOOK

The companion document to the website. The site is the artifact; this is the operating manual for using it — identity rules, the Behance edition, and how to run the recruiter, LinkedIn, print and deployment plays.

---

## 1 · THE POSITIONING

One sentence, memorized, used everywhere (site hero, LinkedIn headline, interviews):

> **An architect who works on both sides of the model — fabricating built fabric for Abu Dhabi's cultural landmarks, and constructing the digital twins that simulate entire cities.**

Why it works: nobody else in the stack of portfolios on a recruiter's desk has *fabricated three Saadiyat museums* **and** *built a country in Unreal Engine*. Every page of the portfolio exists to prove this one sentence. Never dilute it with "passionate, detail-oriented team player" language.

The title block: **Architect · VR & Digital Twin Specialist**. Not "3D artist," not "visualizer" — those undersell the RIBA Part I and the construction record.

## 2 · VISUAL IDENTITY SYSTEM

### 2.1 Concept — "Matter between Model"
The identity holds two worlds in tension: the *physical* (GRC panels, concrete, museum stone) and the *simulated* (UE viewport, GIS coordinates, event markers). Every design decision expresses one side or mediates between them.

### 2.2 Color
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0E1417` | Primary ground — the night-city viewport |
| Ink-2/3/4 | `#131C21 / #1E2A31 / #28353C` | Elevation steps on dark |
| Paper | `#EDE9E1` | Physical-world sections — GRC / bone white |
| Paper-2/3 | `#E4DFD4 / #F4F1EA` | Paper elevation steps |
| Signal | `#4FB7C4` | The single accent — Gulf teal / UI marker cyan. Links, codes, live data |
| Warm | `#C8873F` | Sparse second accent — sodium streetlight amber. Use ≤ 10% as often as Signal |

Rules: dark sections carry the *digital* projects; paper sections carry the *physical and human* content (freelance, profile). Never introduce a third accent. Signal is for wayfinding and data, never for decoration.

### 2.3 Typography
| Role | Face | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **Archivo** (Expanded, `font-stretch:125%`) 700–900 | ALL CAPS, tight leading (0.95), used only for section titles and the name |
| Editorial body | **Spectral** 300–400 | Sentence case, generous line height (1.6+). The narrating voice |
| Data / utility | **Space Mono** | Coordinates, project codes, captions, tags, specs. The GIS voice |

The three faces map to the three registers of the work: the *monument* (Archivo Expanded), the *narrative* (Spectral), the *dataset* (Space Mono). Keep that mapping absolute — a caption is never set in Spectral; a paragraph is never set in Mono.

### 2.4 Grid & spacing
- Max content width **1360px**; 12-column mental grid; images break the text column, text never breaks the image grid.
- Section rhythm: `datum line → title + role tags → spec table → gallery`. Every project follows it — the consistency *is* the luxury.
- White space is a material. If a spread feels empty, it is working.

### 2.5 Signature elements
- **The datum line**: `— P·03 · AL TALL · TRIPOLI ————` a survey-style rule opening every project. It encodes a real archive index, not decoration.
- **Coordinates** in the hero (`N 24.4539° E 54.3773°`) — the digital-twin practice condensed to one line.
- **Codes**: P·01–P·08 professional, A·01–A·09 academic. Use the same codes in print, Behance, and interviews ("Let me show you P·01").

### 2.6 Image treatment
- Full-bleed or grid-aligned; never floated mid-paragraph.
- Captions: Space Mono, lowercase-ish, bottom-left, factual ("factory floor · GRC panel demolding"). No poetic captions.
- Before/after pairs (Sigma & Omega, Mangrove) always presented as pairs — they are the strongest evidence of skill in the archive.
- Never stretch, never watermark, no drop shadows, no rounded corners over 2px.

## 4 · BEHANCE EDITION

Behance rewards one long, image-led scroll. Build **one flagship Behance project, not eighteen**:

**"UAE Digital Twin — Building a Country in Unreal Engine"**
1. Cover: the Ajman/RAK cinematic aerial with UI markers (1400×1120 crop).
2. Opening statement — three sentences from the site's P·01 intro.
3. The pipeline as a full-width graphic: `SATELLITE → 2D FOOTPRINT → 3DS MAX LOW-POLY → UV/TEXTURE → DATASMITH → UNREAL → EVENTS & UI`.
4. Before/after modules (Sigma & Omega, Mangrove) — Behance's format loves these.
5. Reem Island by the numbers: 117 buildings · 11 km² · 8 building classes.
6. Night/dusk stills sequence, then the street-level frames.
7. Close with the CITY PULSE / MOI FM App screens and a credits block.

Then two more projects over the following weeks: **Al Tall** (environmental-analysis carousel is very Behance-friendly) and **Fabricating Saadiyat** (factory photos + GRC drawings). Three excellent projects outrank eighteen thin ones. Tag: `digital twin`, `unreal engine`, `architectural visualization`, `GIS`, `smart city`, `UAE`.

*Sanitization rule*: the two MOI workflow PDFs are marked **Restricted** — the site and Behance use only the assets you supplied as shareable (renders, video stills, described workflow). Before publishing publicly, confirm with your line manager which stills are cleared, and strip any live plate numbers / real-time dashboards if asked.

## 5 · RECRUITER & INTERVIEW STRATEGY

**The 30-second open** (site or print, same script): *"I'm an architect from the fabrication world — I helped produce panels for the Zayed National Museum, the Guggenheim and the Natural History Museum. Now I build the other twin: I model entire Emirates and run them in Unreal Engine for the Ministry of Interior. I work on both sides of the model."* Then open P·01.

**Per-audience emphasis**
- *Design studios (Foster+P, BIG, ZHA, UNStudio)*: lead P·02 (they know those museums; you built them) → P·01 as technology depth → Al Tall as design thinking.
- *Engineering/delivery (AECOM, Atkins, SOM, Gensler)*: lead P·01 pipeline discipline (LOD standards, naming, pivots, versioning) → Reem Island metrics → execution drawings (A·03/A·04 exec sets).
- *Realtime / gaming / digital-twin companies (Epic ecosystem, Bentley, city-tech)*: P·01 exclusively for the first ten minutes — Datasmith workflow, optimization strategy, event/blueprint integration — then the fabrication story as your differentiator.
- *Government innovation labs*: CITY PULSE framing — CIM, IoT, emergency simulation, decision support.

**Interview presentation**: 12 slides max, codes as navigation, one hero image per slide, you narrate the rest. Never read the portfolio to them.

## 6 · LINKEDIN

- **Headline**: `Architect · VR & Digital Twin Specialist — UAE Ministry of Interior | RIBA Part I | Unreal Engine · 3ds Max · GIS`
- **About**: the positioning sentence + three proof lines (museums fabricated, Emirates modeled, UNDP regeneration) + software line. ≤ 120 words.
- **Featured**: pin the portfolio URL, the Behance flagship, and the CV PDF.
- **Experience entries**: reuse the site's spec tables verbatim (role · scope · tools) — consistency across surfaces reads as professionalism.
- Post cadence: one before/after or pipeline graphic per month. Never post restricted MOI material.

---

## 7 · PRINT EDITION

- Format: **A4 landscape, saddle-stitched or perfect-bound, 32–40 pp**, matte 170 gsm inside, 300 gsm soft-touch cover.
- Cover: ink black, name in Archivo Expanded, one embossed datum line. No image — the restraint is the statement.
- Contents = the site's flagship tier only + profile. Academic work gets a single index spread ("A·01–A·09, full archive online").
- Every spread follows the site rhythm (datum → title → spec → imagery) so the print and digital editions are recognizably the same object.
- Print the QR of the live site inside the back cover.

## 8 · DEPLOYMENT

1. GitHub Pages (free): repo → upload folder contents → Settings → Pages → main/root. Live in minutes.
2. Custom domain worth having: `hashemdankar.com` (~$12/yr) → add CNAME in Pages settings.
3. The site is a single HTML file + images — no build step, no dependencies except Google Fonts (falls back to system fonts offline).
4. Before sharing widely: run the URL through Lighthouse once (target 90+ performance; images are already compressed), and test on your phone.
